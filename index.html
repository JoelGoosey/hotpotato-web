Hot Potato

Don't get caught holding it.

Hot Potato is a pass-the-phone party game. A goofy tune plays while you pass the phone around the circle. Nobody knows when the alarm will go off. Whoever is holding it gets burned, then takes the dare. Free to play, no ads, no accounts, nothing leaves your device.
